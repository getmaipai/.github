# MaiPai canonical copy

The one source for product descriptions. Repo description fields, the org
profile, READMEs, and docs pitches all use these, word for word. Change them
here first.

**Org (getmaipai):**
Private, local AI that's actually yours. AI for your home, a robot companion,
and apps to reach them anywhere, all on your own hardware, never the cloud.

**home (MaiPai Home):**
Protect your privacy and your data, save money, and escape the algorithm. Run
your own AI, music, videos, podcasts, maps, books, and more, online or
offline, for real self-reliance, all on your own hardware, for your whole
home.

**desktop (MaiPai Desktop, part of the home repo):**
Mac and Windows app for MaiPai: your private AI chatbot and robot companion,
right on your desktop, integrated with your dock and your computer.

**go (MaiPai Go):**
iPhone and Apple TV app for MaiPai: your private AI chatbot and robot
companion, wherever you are.

**bot (MaiPai Bot):**
A robot friend with its own onboard AI and a personality all its own. It
sees, hears, talks, thinks, and moves independently. Self-charging, so it's
always ready. Holds real conversations, learns and grows from experience, and
remembers the family it lives with.
