</p>

<h3 align="center">Private, local AI that's actually yours.</h3>

<p align="center">
AI for your home, a robot companion, and apps to reach them anywhere,
all running on your own hardware. Nothing leaves your house. No cloud, no
subscriptions, no one else's servers.
</p>

---

**The family:**

- **MaiPai Home**: your family's own AI hub. Chat, music, videos, books,
  podcasts, image making and more, private to your household.
- **MaiPai Desktop**: pins your home companion to the top of your computer
  screen.
- **MaiPai Go**: brings it all to your TV and your phone.
- **MaiPai Bot**: a robot companion for the house (in the workshop).

Built and maintained by [Jesse Torres](https://github.com/JesseWebDotCom).
